🏛 Governance Framework

The GreyMisnomer protocol operates under a layered governance structure modeled after India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS).

  • 🏢 Ministry of Power (MoP) — Policy Direction & Mandate
  • 📋 Steering Committee (NSC-ICM) — Strategic Oversight
    • ⚡ Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) — Methodologies, Accreditation, Targets
  • 🔐 Accredited Validators (ACVAs / VVBs) — Audit, Verify, Sign attestations
  • 💻 Tech Provider (CDAC) — Digital Infrastructure, ZK Node/Tool
  • 🏦 BCI – Registry Owner — Master DB, Interoperability API Bridge
    • 📜 Minting Smart Contract (Issuance Logic)
    • 🔁 Retirement Smart Contract (Settlement Logic)
  • ⚖ CERC / Power Exchanges (IEX/PXIL) — Market & Liquidity (Layer 4)
