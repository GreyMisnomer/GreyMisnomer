RFC-003

🌳 Merkle Commitment

Generate a real commitment over your MRV dataset. This step verifies inclusion and tamper-resistance for the records.

RFC-001

🔏 Issue Proof-of-Integrity

Bind a serial range to your MRV commitment and create the PoI metadata required for minting.

RFC-001 + RFC-002

🏭 Mint Credits

Submit the prepared PoI and mint a serialized credit batch in the client-side registry state.

RFC-002

🔄 Transfer Ownership

Transfer the entire active batch to a new owner address. Ownership updates are reflected in the live registry state.

RFC-002

🔥 Slicing & Burns

Retire active credits by serial sub-range. The registry splits slices and generates a PoO record for each burn.
